## The problem

Getting around Berkeley's campus efficiently (shortcuts, food, finding a library seat) means juggling a lot of apps and word of mouth.

## What I built

CampusFlow is a campus navigation app with shortcut routing, food discovery and library crowd tracking. I designed 4 core user flows in Figma and worked with a 12-person team to turn them into a working product over an 8-week build.

<Screenshot src="/projects/campusflow/flows.png" alt="figma flows" caption="The 4 core user flows in Figma." />

## My role

- **Design:** owned the 4 core user flows in Figma.
- **Research:** led usability testing with 10 students across all 4 flows and turned their feedback into design changes the team prioritized.
- **Backend:** built the Python backend integrating the Google Maps API to power shortcut routing, food discovery and library crowd tracking.
