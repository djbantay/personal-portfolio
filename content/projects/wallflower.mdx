## The problem

Solo event photographers can't watch every corner of a room. If the person you most need a photo of walks in while you're shooting something else, you miss the moment.

## What I built

Wallflower is an AI spotter. A FastAPI backend runs Gemini vision on live camera frames, tracks the guests you've starred, and pushes real-time WebSocket alerts to a React/TypeScript app on your phone.

<Screenshot src="/projects/wallflower/phone-alert.png" alt="phone alert" caption="An alert arriving on the phone when a starred guest is spotted." />

## How it works

- **Vision:** I benchmarked 6 Gemini models and picked one with a 1.56s median latency that got 6/6 test cases right.
- **Reliability:** every call has a timeout and fails over to Gemma 4 running on-device through Ollama (10/10 correct, no API quota).
- **Alert rules:** a guest has to be seen on 2 consecutive ticks before an alert fires, with cooldowns and thresholds that adjust from feedback, so you don't get spammed.
- **Testing:** 37 pytest tests cover the alert logic, plus a go/no-go webcam script with explicit pass criteria.
